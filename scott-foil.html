Hard-Won Battles & Diagnostic Learnings

CLEARANCE TOLERANCES

The Di2 Oval Chainring Dilemma

The Challenge: Shimano electronic front derailleurs are mapped to a circular arc. Installing non-round oval chainrings caused cage rub at the 90° power stroke peak and dropped chains during rapid front shifts under load.

The Solution: Micro-spaced the braze-on clamp to leave 1.0mm vertical clearance at the ring's highest ellipse point, introduced +0.5° outward tail yaw, and tuned E-Tube motor speed limits.

BIOMECHANICAL FIT

Evolutio Sports Physio Fit Protocol

The Challenge: Aggressive aero drops triggered ulnar nerve compression, wrist numbness, and cervical spine fatigue on rides beyond 80km.

The Solution: Full clinical bike fit analysis with Evolutio Physio. Adjusted stem reach by -10.2mm, corrected pelvic tilt over a 143mm saddle bottleneck, and rotated brake hoods inward 7° to match natural ulnar alignment.

TRIBOLOGY & EFFICIENCY

Ultrasonic Waxing vs Melbourne Road Grit

The Challenge: Wet lubricants act as grinding paste with road grit, accelerating chain wear and costing up to 5-8W in drivetrain drag.

The Solution: Multi-stage ultrasonic solvent degreasing (mineral spirits followed by pure 99% isopropyl), followed by hot-melt paraffin/PTFE wax immersion. Results in sub-3W drivetrain friction and zero oily residue.
